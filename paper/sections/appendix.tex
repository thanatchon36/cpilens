The repository rebuilds every number in this paper from public sources. \texttt{make data}
downloads the sources and checks their checksums; \texttt{make models} computes the
embeddings, trains the classifiers and runs the LLMs (cached responses are replayed, so no
API call is made when the cache is present); \texttt{make eval econ} scores the methods on
the reference set and runs the simulator; \texttt{make figures paper} draws the figures and
builds this PDF. \texttt{cpilens report} writes every number
the paper prints into \texttt{paper/numbers.tex}, and a check in continuous integration
fails if the paper's sources contain a number typed by hand.
\texttt{results/run\_manifest.json} records package versions, model revisions and the
random seed (\CfgSeed).

\section{Terms used in the figures}\label{app:glossary}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt, itemsep=0.2em]
\item[OOS] Out of scope: a product of an item that the classifiers do not cover (energy,
services, clothing, durable goods and the like). Sending a product to OOS removes it from
measurement.
\item[Weighted accuracy] The share of the evaluated CPI weight classified correctly,
equation~\eqref{eq:accw}.
\item[Zero-shot] Trained without any Thai label: only on products in other languages.
\item[Weak labels] Labels derived automatically from Open Food Facts categories through the
crosswalk of Appendix~\ref{app:items}, not assigned by a person or by the office.
\item[Official labels] TPSO item codes attached to its price-collection specifications.
\item[kNN, linear, MLP] Three classifiers on top of a fixed text embedding: nearest
neighbours, logistic regression and a neural network with one hidden layer.
\item[Fine-tuned XLM-R] A multilingual encoder whose own parameters are trained on the
labels, rather than a classifier on a fixed embedding.
\item[Cascade] A cheap classifier keeps the products it is confident about and sends the rest
to an LLM.
\item[Core] TPSO's core CPI, which excludes raw food (rice, flour and cereals; meat, poultry
and fish; eggs and dairy products; vegetables and fruit) and energy. In the simulator every
in-scope item outside those four food groups counts as core.
\item[t-SNE] A method that draws high-dimensional embeddings on a plane for display; distances
on the map are only roughly meaningful.
\item[Dirichlet draws] Confusion matrices drawn from the posterior given the reference counts,
used for the intervals of the basis-point errors.
\item[RMSE] Root mean squared error of the simulated inflation rate against the official
rate, over the simulated months.
\end{description}
